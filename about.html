ABOUT US

Clarity Over Complexity

Helping homebuyers and homeowners understand every dollar of their mortgage—without ads, sign-ups, or hidden agendas.

Mortgage Payoff Lab was created to make mortgage math approachable and transparent. Whether you are budgeting for your first home or looking at how extra principal payments can shave years off an existing loan, you deserve clear numbers in seconds.

Too many online financial calculators collect personal contact details before showing full results. We built the opposite: a fast, browser-based workspace where all calculations happen locally on your device.

Privacy-First

No accounts, no tracking of your financial inputs, and no server storage. Every calculation stays in your browser.

Transparent Math

Built on the standard monthly amortization formula with clear assumptions for PITI (Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance) and HOA fees.

Extra Payment Magic

Simulate monthly or one-time extra principal payments to see your exact interest savings and faster payoff timeline.
